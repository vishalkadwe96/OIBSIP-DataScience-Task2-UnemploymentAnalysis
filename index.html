🍸 Key Findings from the Analysis
  • COVID-19 caused a sharp spike in unemployment across all regions in April–May 2020
  • Tripura, Haryana, and Himachal Pradesh top the list of most affected states
  • Urban unemployment rates are significantly higher than rural (avg 12.8% vs 7.2%)
  • Labour participation rate shows negative correlation with unemployment (-0.65)
  • Post-COVID recovery is uneven — some states recovered by Q4 2020, others lagged into 2021
  • The manufacturing and services sectors bore the brunt of job losses
  • Seasonal patterns are visible — unemployment typically rises during Q1 each year
  • States with higher initial employment showed greater absolute job losses during the pandemic

The COVID Cocktail

A timeline of how the pandemic shook India's labour market

JAN 2019 — FEB 2020
The Pre-COVID Era
National unemployment averaged 7.2%. Seasonal fluctuations were normal. Urban rates hovered around 8–9%, while rural rates stayed below 6%.
MAR 2020
The First Lockdown
Nationwide lockdown announced. Unemployment began climbing sharply. Daily wage workers and informal sector hit first. Rate jumped from 8.7% to 23.4% in weeks.
APR — MAY 2020
The Peak
Unemployment hit an all-time high of 27.1% in some states. Urban areas saw rates above 30%. Labour participation collapsed. The bar was officially closed.
JUN — AUG 2020
Unlock 1.0 — 3.0
Gradual reopening. Rates dropped to 8–11% range. Manufacturing resumed. Migrant workers began returning. Rural recovery outpaced urban areas significantly.
SEP 2020 — DEC 2021
The New Normal
Stabilization around 7–9% nationally. Second wave (Apr 2021) caused a smaller spike. Gig economy and digital sectors showed resilience. Structural shifts became visible.
